The app UI is Traditional Chinese only. Screen and button names are given in English with the Chinese label in brackets.

1. WHAT IT IS
Guaxiang is for adults 18+. You scan your own face with the TrueDepth camera, the app measures facial proportions on the device, and compares them with classical Chinese physiognomy books (Mayi Xiangfa, Shenxiang Quanbian, Taiqing Shenjian). It is a reading of traditional culture. The app states that these classical claims have not been verified, and it does not judge health, lifespan, misfortune or character.

2. HOW TO USE (iPhone with Face ID required)
Open the app > Start scan [開始掃描] > tick the two required boxes (I am 18+ and scanning my own face; glasses removed) > Continue [繼續] > allow camera > hold the phone in front of your face for a few seconds > drag the red line to your hairline, or tap Skip [我有瀏海，跳過] > read the report. Tap any card to see the classical text, a plain explanation and the source.
Without TrueDepth (including iPad) the scan screen shows a full-page message that this device cannot scan. The sample report [先看一份範例報告] on the home screen works on any device; it uses made-up numbers and is labelled as a sample.

3. HOW IT DIFFERS FROM OTHER FORTUNE APPS (4.3)
- Proportions are measured from the real face with ARKit. Users never pick their own face shape.
- Every result shows the classical text, a plain explanation and the book it comes from.
- Items that cannot be measured are labelled "not read" [沒看] with the reason. The app does not fill gaps.
- Ask the Book [問古書] may only cite this report. Answers carry evidence tags [依據] that open the cited item. The app removes tags that do not exist in the report and discards answers with no valid tag. Health, off-topic and small-talk questions get fixed text written by us, not model text.

4. FACE DATA
- Collected: ARKit face mesh, blend shapes and depth, plus Vision landmarks, during the scan only.
- Purpose: computing facial proportions for the report.
- Shared with third parties: no. Face data never leaves the device.
- Stored: raw mesh, depth and camera frames are discarded after the scan. The app keeps the measured numbers and one still photo (the most frontal frame) with 2D mark positions, shown at the top of the report. Both stay on the device, with complete file protection, excluded from iCloud backup.
- Retention: until the user taps Settings > Erase All Data [清除所有資料] (two confirmations) or deletes the app.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICE
Ask the Book is optional. Before the first question a consent sheet says the answer is generated by Anthropic's Claude and lists what is sent: the question, recent Q&A, the profile the user typed (birthday converted to age only) and a text summary of the results. Photos, camera frames and face coordinates are not sent. Nothing is sent without consent. Consent can be withdrawn in Settings > Where your data goes [你的資料去了哪裡]. Requests go through our relay server [TBD: relay URL]; the API key is on the relay, not in the app.

6. WHAT THE APP DOES NOT HAVE
No accounts or login. No content visible to other users. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. No photo upload; it only scans the user's own face live. No health assessment.

## 打架的地方（送審前要處理）

1. 相機權限說明（`ios/project.yml:46`）寫「開發工具裡連電腦的時候，也用來掃電腦上的 QR code」，`NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`（`:47`）和 `NSAllowsLocalNetworking` 也都是開發工具用的，而且不在 DEVTOOLS 開關底下。拿掉開發工具時這三項要一起拿掉，不然審查員會看到「連電腦」的權限說明，跟備註第 6 點對不上。
2. 上架指南第九關說拿掉 DEVTOOLS 後範例報告會消失，但首頁的「先看一份範例報告」不在 DEVTOOLS 裡，送審版還會有。我把它寫進備註當成審查員沒有原深感手機時的入口。你要拿掉的話，備註第 2 點最後一句跟著刪。
3. 上架指南第八關第 4 點寫「存在哪：不存」，實際有存一張照片。備註照程式寫。隱私標籤「照片或影片：不收集」還是對的，因為照片沒離開手機（Apple 的「收集」指傳出裝置），但這是我的理解，填後台時再看一次 Apple 的定義。
4. 備註寫「沒有每日運勢」我拿掉了，產品定稿有寫不做，但程式裡沒有地方可以對，留著反而多一句要被檢查的話。
